# e07

## Question

How does `xRangeFilter` interact with multi-column layouts processed by the `multicolumn` preprocessor — do the x-coordinates refer to pre- or post-reflow positions?

## Answer

The docs do not directly address this interaction, and no page explicitly states whether `xRangeFilter` coordinates refer to pre- or post-reflow (pre- or post-multicolumn) positions. Here is what the docs do say about each feature individually, and what can be inferred:

**`xRangeFilter`** (from the Method object page):
- Defines left and right boundaries in which to capture lines, using `start` (left or right edge of the anchor line), `offsetX`, and `width` in inches.
- "This column excludes any lines that partially fall outside the defined rectangular region."
- The docs explicitly describe it as **an alternative** to the Multicolumn preprocessor: "As an alternative to this parameter, use the Multicolumn preprocessor."

**`multicolumn` preprocessor** (from the Multicolumn page):
- Ensures that Sensible sorts lines into columns when present, rather than sorting lines left to right across the page's entire width.
- It affects **line sorting order**, not the physical x-coordinate positions of lines on the page.

**`linearize` preprocessor** (the advanced alternative to `multicolumn`, from the Linearize page):
- "Breaks pages into an array of coordinate-based blocks, before sorting lines."
- Blocks are defined with `minX`, `maxX`, `minY`, `maxY` in inches from the page edges — i.e., physical page coordinates.
- "Any lines that fall partially or completely outside a block are excluded from the preprocessor and are unavailable for SenseML queries."

**Key inference**: Neither `multicolumn` nor `linearize` changes the physical x-coordinates of lines — they only change the order in which lines are sorted/processed. Lines remain at their original page positions. Since `xRangeFilter` filters lines based on their physical position on the page (in inches), and preprocessors only affect sort order rather than physical coordinates, `xRangeFilter` x-coordinates refer to the **original (pre-reflow) page positions**. Reflow here means reordering, not repositioning.

**Practical implication from the docs**: The docs present `xRangeFilter` and the `multicolumn` preprocessor as **alternatives to each other**, not as features designed to be combined. Using both simultaneously is not described or recommended anywhere in the fetched documentation.
