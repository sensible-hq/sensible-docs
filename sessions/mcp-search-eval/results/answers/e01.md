# e01: OCR behavior on mixed PDFs and preprocessor application

## Question

How does Sensible OCR behave on a mixed PDF where some pages are digitally generated and others are scanned — do preprocessors apply uniformly?

## Answer

### Default behavior on mixed PDFs

Sensible does not automatically OCR individual scanned pages within a mixed PDF. Its default pipeline works at the document level:

1. If the PDF contains embedded text, Sensible extracts that text directly and skips OCR entirely for the whole document.
2. If Sensible determines (via heuristics controlled by the OCR Level parameter) that the whole document needs OCR, it OCRs everything.

This means that for a mixed PDF — where some pages are digitally generated and others are scanned — Sensible's automatic heuristics may extract embedded text from the digital pages and silently skip OCR on the scanned pages, producing missing or empty output for the scanned pages.

### How to handle mixed PDFs: the OCR preprocessor

The OCR preprocessor is the purpose-built solution for this scenario. It lets you selectively OCR specific pages within a document rather than OCRing everything or nothing. The docs describe it directly: "This preprocessor is useful when a document contains both embedded text and text images. Selectively OCRing the pages containing text images, rather than the entire document, improves extraction performance."

The preprocessor is configured per-config (not per document type) and supports:

- **`match` + `matchAll`**: OCR the page containing a specific text anchor, or all pages containing that text.
- **`pageOffset`**: OCR a page at a fixed offset from a matched anchor (e.g., the page after a known heading).
- **`pageLinesLimit`**: OCR all pages that have fewer than N embedded text lines — useful when scanned pages have no reliable text anchor and tend to have very few or zero embedded lines.
- **`engine`**: Choose OCR provider (Amazon, Google, Microsoft) per preprocessor instance.

The OCR preprocessor runs after Sensible's whole-document OCR decision step — specifically, it "runs for documents that don't trigger whole-document OCR in a previous step." This means it is the correct mechanism for mixed PDFs: if the document as a whole is not flagged for full OCR, the preprocessor handles the scanned subset.

### Do preprocessors apply uniformly across all pages?

No. Preprocessors are targeted, not uniform. The OCR preprocessor applies only to the pages you specify (by match, offset, or line count threshold). Other preprocessors (Deskew, Merge Lines, Remove Lines, etc.) may apply document-wide depending on their own configuration, but the OCR preprocessor is inherently page-selective by design.

### What you do NOT need to do

If the entire document is a scan or image file, Sensible OCRs it automatically — you do not need to configure the OCR preprocessor in that case.
