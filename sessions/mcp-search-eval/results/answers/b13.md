# Question
Are there prebuilt extractors I can use off the shelf?

# Answer

Yes. Sensible provides a **configuration library** (called the Template library in the UI) with out-of-the-box extraction support for common business document types. You can get started in minutes without writing any custom extraction logic.

## Supported document types (partial list)

- Auto policy declaration pages
- Balance sheets
- Bank statements
- Closing disclosures
- Credit card statements
- Driver's licenses
- Explanation of benefit
- Health insurance claims
- Home policy declaration pages
- Loss runs
- Pay stubs
- Pet policy declaration pages
- Resumes
- Tax forms
- Verifications of employment
- Rent rolls
- Offering memorandums
- And more

## How to use a prebuilt extractor

1. In the Sensible UI, click the **Template library** tab and browse for your document type. Use the **Use case** filter to browse by industry.
2. Click the document type you want. Sensible shows the specific forms supported within that type.
3. Click **Clone to account**. The document type and its configs are added to your **Document types** tab.
4. Test it by uploading sample documents via the **Extract** tab.

If your document type is not in the library, you can build a custom extractor using SenseML — Sensible's query language that combines LLM-based and layout-based extraction methods.
