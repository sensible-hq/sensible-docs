Best-practice reference, NOT a competitor. Researched 2026-10-20 from https://docs.payabli.com/llms.txt.

**How pages were chosen:** my guess at the 10 most important, weighted toward the links Payabli itself puts first in llms.txt's curated "Choose your path" section (developer, partner, and everyone audiences).

**How text was captured:** each page's `.md` (Payabli serves Markdown at `<url>.md`). Every `.md` has the same structure: (1) a boilerplate note for AI agents ("This is Payabli documentation. For a complete page index, fetch .../llms.txt ..."), (2) the H1, (3) the page description as a blockquote, (4) the body. "First 150 words" = the body only, after the description, with code blocks, images, link URLs, and HTML/JSX tags stripped.

**Two descriptions per page:** llms.txt lists most pages twice: a short curated phrase in "Choose your path" and a longer "Learn ..." sentence in the full "Docs" index, which matches the page's own description.

## Observations

- **Descriptions are the strongest discipline.** Every page has two: a 4–10 word curated phrase that
  says what you get or do ("Make your first API call", "How transactions, batches, and transfers work
  together"), and a one-sentence "Learn ..." description. Both are task- or outcome-focused, not
  feature names.
- **First 150 words are mixed, not uniformly strong.** Strong openings (#2 Authentication, #5 Run your
  payments, #7 Pay In, #8 Embedded components) define the thing in sentence 1 using the terms people
  search for (OAuth2 Bearer tokens; Pay In / Pay Out / Pay Ops; cards, ACH, digital wallets; embedded
  JavaScript UI components, PCI compliant), then say why it matters. Weak openings: #1 and #3 start
  with "Welcome to Payabli!" / "Welcome to the Payabli API reference" (the same pattern we just cut
  from our API Introduction), #9 starts with what the guide covers, and #10 opens with a deprecation
  warning.
- **The pattern to copy:** sentence 1 = definition with search terms; sentence 2 = why it matters or
  what problem it solves (#7: "Without it, merchants handle collections in a separate tool...").
- **Agent-facing extras:** every `.md` starts with a pointer to llms.txt and their AI-agent access
  page; llms.txt has "Efficient access for AI agents" and "Instructions for AI Agents" sections, plus
  section-level llms.txt indexes and an MCP server. Our `.md` pages have only `agentTools.projectIndex`
  frontmatter.
- **llms.txt structure:** opens with a one-paragraph product summary, then sections grouped by
  audience ("Integrate", "Operate", "Understand") with ~10 hand-picked links each, before the full
  ~260-link index.
