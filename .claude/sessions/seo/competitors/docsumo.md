# Docsumo: top 20 docs pages and the phrases they target

Researched 2026-10-19. Sites: https://support.docsumo.com (guides under `/docs/`, API reference under `/reference/`). Platform: ReadMe. Total pages in sitemap: 268 URLs (including changelog entries); `llms.txt` lists a curated subset of about 100.
Meta keywords usage: none. 0 of the 45 pages fetched have a `<meta name="keywords">` tag.
Selection criteria: I prioritized concept and feature pages that map to Sensible topics (overview, Document AI, accuracy, supported documents, ingestion, LLM field/table extraction, table OCR, auto-classify, auto-split, confidence scores, validation, review screen, image pre-processing, webhook, API intro, MCP). I picked the newer, fuller page when a topic had both a full guide and a `-1` FAQ stub, except where only the stub covers the topic (Table Vision, Field Level Validation, Image Pre-Processing). The representative per-document-type pages are Invoice and Bank Statement Analytics; the sitemap has about 40 of these pages (AI-Models HUB, plus legacy `-1` duplicates). I left out billing, SSO/2FA, user management, the per-tool MCP setup pages (Claude Code, Cursor, and others, which are short and near-duplicate), changelog, and the Agents/Case pages, which have no Sensible equivalent.
Method: I extracted the title, description, H1, and H2s verbatim from the raw HTML. ReadMe truncates descriptions to about 300 chars, and I truncated them further to 200 here. The `/reference/` pages have no real H2s, only sidebar section names. Phrases were checked by script: each one appears literally in the title, description, H1, or H2s, and "Borrowable" means the phrase is missing from the mapped Sensible file (frontmatter stripped, case-insensitive).

## Patterns
- Their titles are mostly feature names prefixed with "AI" or "LLM" (AI Auto Classifier, AI Split, LLM-Powered Auto-Extract Fields, AI Extraction Reasoning & Confidence Scores). Only a few titles describe tasks ("Key-Value Field Extraction Using LLM Prompts", "Prompt-Based Table Extraction", "How to increase extraction accuracy to 95+"). The task-style titles are the ones that carry search phrases.
- The same umbrella phrases come up again and again: "document AI", "intelligent document processing", "automate data capture", "unstructured data into structured data", "manual data entry", and "AI-powered". Sensible's bodies use almost none of these. Even `idp.md` lacks "document AI" and "data capture".
- "Prompt" is their main word for LLM configuration: "LLM prompts", "prompt-based table extraction", "column-specific prompts", and "split prompt". Sensible says "query" or "description", so the "prompt-based X" searches go to them.
- They name things after the user's goal: "extraction accuracy", "confidence scores", "review screen", "validate the extracted data", and "document ingestion". Sensible's matching pages use other words ("Qualifying LLM accuracy", "Human review", "Validating extractions") and miss these exact strings.
- Their SEO hygiene is poor. Many meta descriptions are the opening FAQ text ("1. What is the Review Screen in Docsumo?"), several titles are generic ("Introduction", "Webhook", "Invoice"), and there are duplicate `-1` stubs for almost every feature. Sensible can outrank them on the phrases listed here with deliberate titles and excerpts, not just body mentions.
- The MCP section is task-shaped and lists AI clients by name (Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Gemini CLI, VS Code, Windsurf). Sensible's `mcp.md` already names Claude and Cursor but not ChatGPT.
