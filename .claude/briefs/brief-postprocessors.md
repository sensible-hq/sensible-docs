## Who is reading this page

**Role:** Developers integrating Sensible extraction into an application; occasionally customer success engineers helping a customer map Sensible output to a downstream system.

**How they arrive:** Via the left-nav (exploring what postprocessors are), or from a cross-reference in JsonLogic or Custom Computation docs.

**Subscription context:** Any tier; postprocessors are available across plans.

**Vocabulary:** Comfortable with JSON and APIs. May not know what "postprocessor" means in Sensible's terminology yet — this page defines it.

**What they know:** They've extracted data and have a `parsed_document` — now they want to reshape it. They may have an existing schema their downstream app expects.

**What they don't know:** Whether there's a postprocessor type that fits their target format (JSON schema, XML, etc.).

## What they're trying to do

Pick the right postprocessor type for their integration. They're not reading this page deeply — they want to scan the options and click into the right child page.

## Questions they expect this page to answer

- What is a postprocessor and where does its output appear?
- What postprocessor types are available?
- Which type should I use for my use case (arbitrary JSON schema vs. XML)?

## What belongs

- A one-sentence definition of what postprocessors do and where the output lands
- A table of postprocessor types with descriptions short enough to scan
- The Excel output limitation (it's a constraint that affects integration decisions)

## What does NOT belong

- Parameter details (those belong on child pages)
- Examples (child pages)
- Explanation of JsonLogic (link to it, don't repeat it)
